## Milestone 0: Hardware Design and Testing

### What this has entailed
1. Motors + Wheel combos characterized, physical parameters calculated, low-pass filter tuned for velocity feedback.
2. Initial rev of hardware mounts (rails and wheel module motor mounts) printed and assembled on box.
3. Mounts for basic power circuitry designed; fuses, fuse holders, and battery connectors have been acquired.

**Detailed write-up in progress, stay tuned!**

### Room for improvement (future milestones)
1. Tweaking of mounting clip and plate dimensions for better assembly experience.
2. Redesign of rail connections and modules to reduce need for fasteners and atypical/specialized equipment (such as saws and deburring tools).
