## Milestone 7: Hardware Design Revisions

### What this will entail
1. Tweaking of mounting clip and plate dimensions for better assembly experience.
2. Redesign of rail connections and modules to reduce need for fasteners and atypical/specialized equipment (such as saws and deburring tools).
